# Bananarang 🍌

**Throw a banana. It comes back. Everything in its path does not.**

The Bananarang is a throwable banana that flies like a real boomerang — it
carves a wide curving arc through the world, *destroying every breakable
block it touches*, knocking the life out of anything alive along the way,
and then sails right back into your inventory like nothing happened.

## ✨ Features

- 🍌 **One item. No crafting. No mercy.** Find the Bananarang in the
  *Tools & Utilities* creative tab, or `/give @s bananarang:banana`.
- 🪃 **True boomerang flight** — a circular arc roughly 15 blocks across,
  curving left like a right-handed throw, with homing return if you move.
- 💥 **Total terrain liberation** — every block it touches is destroyed
  (drops included). Bedrock survives. Your base might not.
- ⚔️ **5 hearts of damage** to any mob or player in its path.
- 🔁 **It always comes back** — straight into your inventory, or dropped at
  your feet if you're full.

## ⚠️ A word of caution

This is a chaos toy. It does not distinguish between "enemy fortress" and
"the house you spent four hours building". Throw responsibly — or don't.

## 📦 Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2 (Java Edition)
- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/)
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)

Required on both client and server.
